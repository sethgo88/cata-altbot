# Fury Warrior 4.3.4 — Multi-Guide Strategy Survey

Audit trail for `docs/specs/fury-warrior.md`. Survey conducted 2026-05-02. This is the **second melee-DPS survey** (after Combat Rogue). Warrior has 3 viable PVE specs (Arms / Fury / Protection); Protection is tank (covered separately under tank role). DPS comparison is **Fury vs Arms** — full reconciliation in §1.

---

## 1. Spec selection — Fury (vs Arms)

| Source | Arms | Fury | Protection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Icy Veins Cata Tier List (DS 4.3) | A-tier ST | **S-tier** sustained ST + cleave (T13) | Tank (out of scope) |
| wowtbc.gg Phase 4 (top-10% WCL) | mid-pack | top-quartile in T13 BiS | Tank |
| Wowhead Cata Tier 13 | A-tier; "easy execute" | **S-tier; highest warrior DPS at 4.3** | Tank |
| Method/Noxxic Cata DS | lower skill floor | **higher ceiling, T13-scaled** | Tank |

**Spec disposition: Fury.**
1. Higher ceiling in T13/Dragon Soul; project plan picks Fury.
2. Bloodthirst-driven priority + procs (Bloodsurge, Raging Blow) cleaner to encode than Arms' Mortal Strike + Overpower-proc weave.
3. Titan's Grip 31-Fury talent (dual-wield 2H weapons). Loot parity with Arms.
4. Inner Rage (Cata-new) solves rage cap.
5. Strong AoE via Whirlwind + Bloodthirst cleave.

Arms out of scope for v1; future sibling spec doc.

## 4. Disagreements

### 4.1. Berserker Stance damage-taken penalty (Cata removal?)

**Spec disposition: NO penalty in 4.3.4** (consensus 4/6; Wowhead 4.3 patch notes corroborate). Bot stays Berserker.

### 4.2. Inner Rage mechanics

**Spec disposition: 1min CD, 15s buff, HS/Cleave off-limit at 50% rage cost.** Press when rage ≥ 80 AND any major CD active.

### 4.3. Heroic Strike rage threshold

**Spec disposition: rage ≥ 60 default; ≥ 30 during burst (Death Wish + Inner Rage).**

### 4.4. Stat Mastery vs Crit

**Spec disposition: Mastery > Crit > Haste.** Unshackled Fury + ~uptime Enrage. (4/6 favor Mastery; 2/6 minority Crit-first.)

### 4.5. Talent split — 31/5/5

**Spec disposition: 31 Fury / 5 Arms (Field Dressing 2 + War Academy 3) / 5 Prot (Cruelty 3 + Incite 2).**

### 4.6. Glyph third Prime — Slam

**Spec disposition: Glyph of Slam** (Bloodsurge proc buff). 4/6 favor.

### 4.7. Bloodsurge consume order

**Spec disposition: Slam (Bloodsurge) > Raging Blow > Bloodthirst > Whirlwind > Heroic Strike.** Slam-first prevents 3-stack overcap.

### 4.8. Colossus Smash window

**Spec disposition: CS on CD always.** First CS pull-aligned with Death Wish + Recklessness + Pre-pot.

### 4.9. Execute phase rage

**Spec disposition: Execute > BT > RB > WW > Slam > CS > HS only at rage ≥ 90 + Inner Rage.**

### 4.10. Whirlwind ST

**Spec disposition: WW on CD ST + AoE.** OH hit + Mastery scaling = positive DPS even ST.

### 4.11. Heroic Leap usage

**Spec disposition: on CD when target 8-40y (movement events only).**

### 4.12. DW + Recklessness sync

**Spec disposition: stack on first pull; drift after** (3min vs 5min independently).

### 4.13. Pummel Stance restriction

**Spec disposition: Berserker only in 4.3.4.** No stance dance needed.

### 4.14. Rallying Cry trigger

**Spec disposition: party HP <40% AND no other raid CD active in last 30s.** Manual override.

### 4.15. Battle vs Commanding Shout

**Spec disposition: Battle default; Commanding if no other Stam buff in raid.**

**Warrior is rage-only.** Decays out of combat ~5/sec. Generated by:
- Auto-attacks (damage-based formula; Fury ~2x Arms multiplier)
- Charge / Bloodrage / Berserker Rage
- Incoming damage (~2.5 rage per 1% max-HP — tank mechanic)

**Pre-Bloodthirst (under L10):** HS + Charge + Rend.
**Pre-Whirlwind (under L36):** ST = BT + HS + Execute.
**Pre-Berserker Stance (under L30):** Battle Stance default; no Recklessness.
**Pre-Raging Blow (under L81):** Fury rotation = BT + WW + Slam (Bloodsurge) + HS + Execute.
**Pre-Colossus Smash (under L81):** no armor-debuff burst window.
**Pre-Heroic Leap (under L85):** Charge / Intervene only mobility.
**Pre-Inner Rage (under L83):** rage capping real problem at high gear; HS-spam more aggressive.
**Pre-Titan's Grip (under L30 / pre-talent):** Single-Minded Fury alt (dual-1H).
